The site uses Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree. Per the owner's requested update, this portal instead uses native Apple-style typography: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. Apple systems render their installed San Francisco UI font. No Apple font files are distributed; other devices use their native fallback. Section headings also use this premium neutral sans, replacing the original serif direction.

The neighborhood chart palette remains the six explicit colors from the original portal specification. The requested gold sub-lines remain subtle, while the hero accent follows the current website's peony branding. Motion is only a maximum three-pixel scroll-linked headline drift and brief control feedback, all disabled for reduced motion.

The agency support number is taken from the website, not invented. All clinic and lead sample contacts remain fictional. The logo belongs to its owner; this ZIP does not grant third-party reuse rights.
